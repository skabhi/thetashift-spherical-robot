Robot task timeline

Open the trace.json copied from your ESP32. Each row shows one task; left to right is execution order. Bars measure elapsed callback time, including any interrupt or garbage collection pauses. Gaps include scheduler work and waiting, not necessarily CPU idle time.

Load a trace, then hover over a bar for exact timestamps.

Very short events are drawn at least two pixels wide for visibility; hover for their measured duration. This is an application step trace, not an RTOS context-switch trace. Trace collection itself adds overhead.
